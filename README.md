## Overview
Technoksha is a premier engineering technology partner based in Bengaluru, offering a comprehensive suite of CAD/CAM solutions and high‑performance workstations. Our expertise spans:

- **ZWSOFT ZWCAD** – powerful 2‑D/3‑D drafting and modeling.
- **Siemens NX** – advanced product design, simulation, and manufacturing.
- **Hexagon ESPRIT** – industry‑leading CAM for CNC machining.
- **Reverse Engineering** – rapid digitization of physical parts.
- **Professional Workstations** – optimized hardware configurations for demanding engineering workloads.

We empower manufacturers, product developers, and design studios to accelerate time‑to‑market while maintaining the highest standards of precision and quality.

## Key Features
- End‑to‑end workflow integration across CAD, CAM, and simulation tools.
- Customizable workstation builds tuned for each software stack.
- Expert consulting, training, and support services.
- Proven track record delivering solutions for aerospace, automotive, and industrial sectors.
